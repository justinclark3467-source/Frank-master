Dearest Ma,
I went back to the grove.
I told myself I would not, but the intercepted message changed everything. If they know about the network, if they are trying to shut it down, then she is in danger. And I could not sit here and do nothing.
I went at night, the way I always did. The path was the same, the stones were the same, but this time something was different. The marks were not just visible — they were active. Freshly cleaned, the circle and line standing out against the old stone.
Someone was there. Recently.
I followed the marks, deeper than I had ever gone. The tunnel entrance was not where I expected it to be, hidden behind a section of wall that looked like it had not moved in centuries. But it moved for me. Behind it, stairs led down into darkness.
I could hear things. Voices, faint and far away. The sound of movement, of people who are trying to stay quiet. The underground was not just a passage. It was alive.
I went down. Slowly, carefully, the way the book taught me. And then I saw her.
Lucia.
She was there, in a chamber that opened up from the tunnel, surrounded by others. Italians, some of them. And Germans — not soldiers, but civilians. Hidden families. Children. People who had run from the war and found their way here.
She looked at me and I looked at her and neither of us spoke for a long moment. Then she smiled, just slightly, and said, "I knew you'd come back."
She told me everything. The network, the tunnels, the resistance that has been here for generations. This is not just a hiding place. It is an escape route. A way for people who want out to get to safety. To Switzerland. To places the Germans cannot reach.
She has been part of it all along. Her family, her people, they built this. And now she is the one who keeps it running.
The Germans found a section of the tunnel yesterday. They do not know where it leads, but they are getting closer. She needs help, and she asked for mine.
I said yes.
I do not know what happens next. I do not know if I will be able to come back from this. But I know that this is right. This is why I am here.
I am going to help her. And when it is over, I will come home to you.
Do not worry about me. I am doing what is right.
Your loving son,
Frank
