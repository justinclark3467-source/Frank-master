### 32. Russo joke pattern ("got a laugh out of...") — 4 files
**Files:** Ma 3, 4, 5, 6
**Variations:**
- Ma 3: "that got a laugh out of everyone close enough to hear"
- Ma 4: "That got a few quiet laughs, enough to break the tension for a moment"
- Ma 5: "That got a quiet laugh out of a few of us"
- Ma 6: "It got a laugh out of a few of us, even Russo once he caught his breath"
**Issue:** Same beat in 4 consecutive letters — Russo does something dumb, someone quips, laugh
**Alternatives:**
- Skip the laugh beat entirely — describe tension breaking without naming it
- Have a character besides Russo be the butt of the joke
- Let the joke land and move on — don't narrate the laugh
- Use a gesture instead: "Sal just shook his head and kept walking"
- Describe the silence *before* the joke, not the laugh *after*
- Different character each time: Petey makes the joke, not just Sal
- Vary the response: "Russo didn't get it, but the rest of us did"
- No punchline needed — just describe the mood shifting
