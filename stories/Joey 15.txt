Joey,

The store is quiet tonight. Too quiet. Ma is asleep, and I am sitting behind the counter with the lights off, watching the street outside. There is a man who has been walking past the store for the past three nights. Same time. Same pace. He stops for a moment, looks at the door, and keeps moving.

I know who he is. The Greek sent him. I am sure of it.

Things have been strange since Frank left. The store kept running, and I waited. That is what Frank told me to do. Wait. Watch. Let him make the first move.

But the Greek is getting impatient. He has buildings on Flatbush Avenue, places he took the same way he is trying to take ours. I hear things. I listen.

I got a letter from Frank last month. War news — not much, just that he is alive. He mentioned a girl. Lucia. I could tell she matters. I hope she is keeping him safe.

But I cannot think about that now. I have my own war to fight. Here. At home.

There is something I have not told anyone. A few weeks ago, I found a letter slipped under the door. No name, no signature. Just a few lines: "The store will be mine soon. You can either get out of the way or get hurt." I burned it. Did not tell Ma. Did not tell anyone.

But I remembered what Frank said. The panel behind the shelves. The loose floorboard. The back window. He knew, even then, that something like this might happen. He prepared me. And I am glad he did.

I checked them last week. The panel behind the shelves — there is a revolver there. Old but clean. Maintained. And under the floorboard, a roll of bills. Enough to get us through if things go bad. Enough to disappear if we have to.

I do not want to use them. But I will if I have to. I am not a violent man, but I am not a fool either. The Greek thinks we are weak because Frank is gone. He thinks we cannot fight back.

He is wrong.

The man walked past again tonight. Stopped a little longer this time. I am ready. I have the gun on me right now, tucked where I can reach it. And I have my eyes open. Frank told me to watch who comes and goes, who asks questions. I have been watching.

Tomorrow I am going to talk to Mr. Caruso. He has a brother in the police. I am not going to the Greek directly. I am going around him. That is how you handle a man like that. You build your own ground before he tries to take it from under you.

Stay sharp.

Frank
