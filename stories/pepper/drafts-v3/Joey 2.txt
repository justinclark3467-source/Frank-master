Joey,

The rain has not let up here, and everything feels heavier because of it. Ground, gear, even the air. You get used to it after a while, but you never forget it is there. It slows everything down just enough to make a man think twice about every step, and sometimes that is the difference between getting through something and not.

I went back to the villa. The book is still with me, and I have been going through it whenever I get the chance. I am telling you now it is not just a collection of drawings. Those markings in the margins line up with something — not roads, not anything official. I cannot figure out what yet. Maybe a garden? A map of properties? I am just guessing.

And I saw someone again.

Not just a glimpse this time, but enough to know she is real and that she moves through that grove like she knows every inch of it. She does not hesitate. That is not something you fake, not here. The way she moved — like she was disappearing into something.

I checked the area after she passed. Took it slow. There are spots where the ground changes — slight dips, places where water runs underneath, areas that feel different from the surface.

I keep studying the book. The more I look at it, the less I understand. Some of the markings look like herbs — maybe it is some kind of gardening book? Some look like directions, but to where? There is one page missing entirely, torn out clean. Someone wanted to hide whatever was on that page.

The book could be nothing. A old woman's journal, a merchant's record, someone's collection of remedies. Or it could be something. I do not know yet.

How is the store? How is Ma? I think about you both more than I should.

Write when you can. Tell me what is going on back home. Even the small things.

Russo almost got himself killed last week. He tried to set up a card game in the middle of a firefight. Sal had to tackle him into a ditch. The man has no sense, but he keeps us laughing.

We moved to a new position yesterday. Higher ground, better view. But the Germans are still out there, watching. You never stop feeling watched over here.

I keep thinking about the book. There is something there, I am sure of it. But I cannot piece it together yet. Maybe I never will.

Stay sharp.

Frank
