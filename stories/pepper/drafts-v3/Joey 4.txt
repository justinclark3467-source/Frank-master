Joey,

It is starting to make a little more sense now.

The book marks where the ground is stable. Not roads, not paths, but places where the earth will hold a man's weight and places where it will not. You follow the markings right, and you can move through places other men would never risk.

I tested it myself. Not guessing. Not following someone else. I took what I could from the book and matched it against the land. Every step exactly where the markings said.

The ground held. No sink, no shift, nothing to give me away.

That is not luck. That is something someone knew.

The marks line up with it. That circle with the line — it is not decoration. It is a marker. You see it, and you know where you are in relation to the stable ground.

Which means someone made this. Someone who knew these hills. Someone who wanted others to be able to move through them safely.

Or maybe I am wrong. Maybe it is just a farmer's record. A land survey. Something ordinary. I keep telling myself that, but something in my gut says otherwise.

She knows it too. I can tell by the way she moves through that grove. She does not need the book. She has known these places her whole life. And when she looked at me the last time I saw her, she knew I was starting to see it too.

I am not telling anyone else about this. Not yet. There is too much I do not understand, and I could be wrong about all of it.

Maybe the book is nothing more than an old farmer's record. Maybe I am seeing patterns that are not there. But something tells me otherwise. Something in the way she moves, in the way the ground changes, in the way this place feels different from everywhere else.

How is the store? How is Ma? I know you are holding things down.

Write when you can. Tell me about home.

I think about the store more than I should. About the smell of coffee in the morning, about the sound of the bell when someone walks in. About you behind the counter, arguing with someone about the price of something that does not matter. About Ma in the kitchen, making breakfast before anyone is awake.

I am getting closer to understanding the book, but every answer brings more questions. Maybe that is how it is supposed to be.

Stay sharp.

Frank
