Joey,
I went back to the place I told you about, the grove near the broken wall. I took my time getting there, moved the way I have been learning to, checking each step and watching the ground more than the path ahead. The rain has changed the land just enough that you cannot trust what you remember from before, so I treated it like I had never seen it at all.
She was not there.
I did not expect that to sit with me the way it did, but it did. I walked the same stretch I had followed before, checked the edge of the trees, the wall, and the ground that holds firmer than the rest. At first, there was nothing to see, nothing that stood out. It looked like any other part of this place that has been passed over too many times.
Then I slowed down.
That is when I started to notice the differences. The ground near the wall had been disturbed, not enough to be obvious, but enough that it did not match the rest. One of the stones had shifted slightly, just enough to expose clean earth beneath it where everything else is packed down from the rain. Someone had been there, and not long before I arrived.
I checked the mark again, the circle with the line through it. Still there, still clean, still placed where it means something, even if I do not fully understand it yet. That part has not changed.
What has changed is how I am looking at it.
I went back through the book after that, took my time with it in a way I had not before. The drawings, the notes, the markings in the margins, they are not random. They line up with what is under your feet, not what you see in front of you. That is the part I am starting to understand.
But not enough.
Not yet.
And I am not going to rush it. That is how a man ends up stepping where he should not.
Back home, you keep things exactly the way they are. Do not let the Greek think he has room to move just because things feel quiet. Men like him wait for that kind of moment. They mistake patience for weakness, and that is when they show themselves.
You do not give him that.
You watch who comes around. You watch who starts asking questions they never cared about before. That will tell you what he is doing before he says a word about it.
I will write again when I have something worth telling you.
Frank
