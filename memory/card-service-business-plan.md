# Card Reminder Service - Business Plan
## "Never Forget" Card Service

### COST STRUCTURE (Per Card Mailed)
| Item | Cost |
|------|------|
| Card stock (nice cardstock) | $0.35 |
| Printing (full color) | $0.25 |
| Envelope | $0.10 |
| LetterStream first class | $1.23 |
| **Total cost per card** | **$1.93** |

---

### PRICING TIERS

#### Tier 1: "Thoughtful" (1 Recipient)
- 2 cards/year (Birthday + Anniversary)
- 2 mailings
- Price: **$9.99/month** ($120/year)

#### Tier 2: "Family" (3 Recipients)
- 6 cards/year (2 per person x 3)
- 6 mailings
- Price: **$19.99/month** ($240/year)

#### Tier 3: "Connected" (5 Recipients)
- 10 cards/year (2 per person x 5)
- 10 mailings
- Price: **$29.99/month** ($360/year)

#### Tier 4: "Community" (10 Recipients)
- 20 cards/year
- 20 mailings
- Price: **$49.99/month** ($600/year)

### CROSS-SELL OPPORTUNITIES

---

### BREAK-EVEN WITH $500

If we price at Family tier ($19.99):
- Each subscriber = $8.41 profit
- $500 ÷ $8.41 = **60 subscribers to break-even**
- Very achievable in Month 1!

---

### COMPETITIVE ADVANTAGE vs GroupGreeting

- They charge per card individually (~$3-5/card)
- Our subscription is CHEAPER for 3+ recipients
- Better for families, not just individuals
- Can undercut on price AND make margin
