# Sales Follow-Up Questions Framework

When a business declines our marketing services, use this script to gather intelligence:

---

## Question 1: The "Why"
> "Would you mind sharing why you're saying no?"

Purpose: Understand the real objection

---

## Question 2: The "What Would Have Needed"
> "What would have needed to be included for you to say yes?"

Purpose: Understand what features/services would have moved them

---

## Question 3: If Price/Cost (Follow-Up)
> "What would have needed to have been included to have justified the cost?"

Purpose: Understand if it's truly price or if they'd pay more for more value

---

## Why This Matters
- Helps us improve our offering
- Identifies gaps in our service
- Reveals pricing sensitivity
- Provides market intelligence

---

## Internal Note
This is for internal learning — not to debate or argue with the prospect. Just gather info and thank them for their feedback.
