### Report Sending Schedule
- **Sent:** Every Monday morning
- **Data range:** Previous week (Monday-Sunday)
- **Auto-generated:** Based on platform analytics + link tracking

---

### Key Principles
1. **Show numbers** — not just "I posted something"
2. **Ask for business** — "did you get customers?" every time
3. **Provide context** — compare to previous week
4. **Make it actionable** — what's coming next week
5. **Invite reply** — two-way conversation, not one-way report
