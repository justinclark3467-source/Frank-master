# DRAFT - Cold Outreach Email Template
## Version 1.0 - MainStreet Media

---

### Subject Line Options
- Free social media content for [Business Name]?
- Boost your leads with zero effort
- [Business Name] — let me show you what's possible

---

### The Email (300 words max)

**Subject:** Free social media content for [Business Name]?

---

Hey [First Name],

I noticed [Business Name] doesn't have much of an online presence — and that's costing you neighbors searching for help.

Most [industry] companies I see online have either no social media or posts that haven't been updated in months. Meanwhile, their competitors are showing up everywhere.

I'm with MainStreet Media. We create content for local businesses — completely free to start. No catch.

Here's what I'll do for you:
- 2-3 social media posts (different styles)
- See what resonates with YOUR customers
- Track the results (clicks, calls, leads)

If the posts bring you leads — great, we talk about continuing at $29/month. If not — you lost nothing.

Want to see what I'd create for you?

— [Your Name]
MainStreet Media

P.S. — No time needed from you. I do the work, you just approve.

---

### Follow-Up Sequence

**Day 3 (if no reply):**
"Hey [Name], just checking if you got my email about the free content. Let me know if you're interested — happy to show you what's possible!"

**Day 7 (final):**
"I get it — you're busy. I'll leave you alone after this. But if you ever want help with your social media,reply here and I'll be ready. Cheers!"

---

### Key Principles (From Expert Research)
1. **Short subject line** — under 50 characters
2. **Personalize** — use their name, their business
3. **Lead with their pain** — "it's costing you"
4. **No pitchy language** — "let me show you" not "buy now"
5. **Free value first** — prove it before asking
6. **Low commitment** — "you lost nothing"
7. **Clear CTA** — single next step
