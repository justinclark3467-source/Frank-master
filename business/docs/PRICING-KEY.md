| Content Type | Description | When to Choose |
|--------------|-------------|----------------|

## HOW PRICING WORKS

### Option A: Choose a Tier
Pick the tier that closest matches your needs. Add any extra services as add-ons.

### Option B: Build Your Own (Ala Carte)
If you want specific services that don't fit a tier, we can quote ala carte:
- Posts: $X/post (varies by content type)
- Platforms: $15/platform/month
- Tracking: $10/month
- Review response: $25/month
- Email sequences: $50-100 one-time
- etc.

### We Compare & Recommend
We'll always show you: (1) Ala carte total, (2) Closest tier + add-ons, and recommend whichever is cheaper.
