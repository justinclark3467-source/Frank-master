# STORY IDEA 1: THE LAST WEIGHT

## Genre: Fantasy / Western / Weird Fiction

### Premise
In the town of Leadville, Colorado, 1879, gravity is not constant. People called "Heavies" can increase or decrease the weight of objects - and themselves. The more weight you can control, the more powerful you are. Mining companies pay fortunes for Heavies who can lighten ore for transport, or make drilling equipment heavy enough to break through bedrock.

The twist: EVERYONE has a "weight limit" - the maximum weight they can affect in their lifetime. Once reached, they can never use their power again. Many Heavies spend their entire lives hovering at their limit, terrified to use their gift.

### Main Character
**MAREN COLE** - A 28-year-old former Heavy whose brother was the most powerful in the territory - until he reached his limit and committed suicide rather than live as an ordinary man. She carries his final "weight" (a railroad spike he was manipulating when he died) fused to her palm. She's learned to hide her ability entirely, working as a blacksmith. When a mine collapse traps 200 workers, she's the only one who can save them - but using her power will cost her everything.

### The Conflict
The mine owner refuses to pay for a proper rescue, planning to let the workers die to avoid liability. Maren must decide: use her power to save them and lose her ability forever, or walk away and let them die. But there's a deeper secret - her brother's "suicide" was actually murder. He was killed because he was about to expose that the mining companies have been deliberately causing collapses to collect insurance. Now the same people who killed him are watching her.

### Unique Magic System
- Heavies can make things heavier or lighter, but every use counts against their lifetime limit
- The closer to their limit, the more physically painful using their power becomes
- At the limit, a Heavy becomes "null" - completely normal, no ability whatsoever
- Some Heavies sell their remaining weight to others (creating incredibly powerful but dying Heavies)
- The visual effect shows as a faint distortion around affected objects

### Themes
- The cost of power and what we sacrifice for it
- Class struggle (mining companies vs workers)
- What makes us special - our abilities or our choices?
