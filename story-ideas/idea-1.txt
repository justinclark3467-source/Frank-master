# STORY IDEA 1: THE POTENTIAL TRADE

## Genre: Fantasy / Dark Social Commentary

### Premise
In the kingdom of Valdren, every person is born with a finite amount of "Potential" - a visible, quantifiable energy that determines how skilled they can become at any pursuit. Potential glows faintly in a person's eyes and can be measured by specialized Seers. The more Potential you have, the greater your capacity for excellence in battle, magic, art, or intellect.

The controversial practice of Potential Trading has made Valdren the wealthiest kingdom in the world. The ultra-rich can purchase Potential from the desperate poor, becoming superhuman in their abilities while the sellers fade into mediocrity. The poor trade their futures for immediate survival.

### Main Character
**CIRA VOSS** - A 19-year-old Seer apprentice who can measure Potential but has almost none herself. She's resigned to a life of mediocrity until she discovers she has an extremely rare ability: she can SEE Potential transfers happening - even the illegal black market ones that happen in shadow. When she witnesses a noble murdering a family to steal their Potential (a practice called "bleeding"), she becomes the only person who can expose the kingdom's darkest secret.

### The Conflict
The King himself built his empire on "Blood Potential" - murdering citizens to absorb their Potential. He appears to have centuries of life remaining because of it. Cira must navigate a world where the powerful buy and sell human potential, facing the question: if everyone traded their Potential away, would humanity eventually become... nothing?

### Unique Elements
- Potential appears as colored light in the eyes (everyone's color is different based on their nature)
- The act of trading Potential is physically painful and leaves "ghost shadows" of the donor's last emotions
- There's a resistance movement called "The Hollow" - people who sold all their Potential and became walking husks that can still feel but not achieve anything
- Potential cannot be created, only transferred - meaning the total Potential in the world is decreasing as the wealthy horde it

### Themes
- Class inequality as literal life-stealing
- The cost of "optimization" and enhancement
- What makes us human - our achievements or our potential?

### Word Count Target
Full novel - 80,000-100,000 words
